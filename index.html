About Version 2.0 PC

Version 2.0 PC buys used computer parts, tests, cleans, and refurbishes them to give them a second life. We sell individual components or use them to build affordable gaming and everyday PCs, providing reliable hardware while reducing electronic waste.


Our goal is to make PC hardware more affordable while helping keep usable electronics out of landfills.
